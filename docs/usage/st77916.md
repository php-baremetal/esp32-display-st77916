# St77916

`Baremetal\Spi\Driver\St77916` extends `Baremetal\Spi\Device` and implements `Baremetal\Output\Display`. Colours
are RGB565 integers: 5 bits red, 6 green, 5 blue, `0x0000` black, `0xFFFF` white.

## Constructor

<!-- @method name="new St77916(Bus $bus, int $cs = 21, int $rst = 3, int $bl = -1, int $width = 360, int $height = 360, int $x_gap = 0, int $y_gap = 0, bool $mirror_x = false, bool $mirror_y = false, bool $swap_xy = false, bool $invert = false)" visibility="public" -->

<!-- @params -->
<!-- @param name="$bus" type="Bus" required -->
A `Baremetal\Spi\Bus` opened as QSPI: pass `sclk`, `mosi` (data 0), `miso` (data 1), `data2` and `data3`.
<!-- @endparam -->
<!-- @param name="$cs" type="int" default="21" -->
Chip-select GPIO.
<!-- @endparam -->
<!-- @param name="$rst" type="int" default="3" -->
Reset GPIO, pulsed at construction and by `wake()`. `-1` when the reset line is not wired to a GPIO.
<!-- @endparam -->
<!-- @param name="$bl" type="int" default="-1" -->
Backlight GPIO, driven high once the panel is on. `-1` leaves the backlight alone.
<!-- @endparam -->
<!-- @param name="$width" type="int" default="360" -->
Width of the drawing area in pixels, 1 to 4096: the panel's width, or its height with `$swap_xy`.
<!-- @endparam -->
<!-- @param name="$height" type="int" default="360" -->
Height of the drawing area in pixels, 1 to 4096: the panel's height, or its width with `$swap_xy`.
<!-- @endparam -->
<!-- @param name="$x_gap" type="int" default="0" -->
Column offset of the visible area in the controller's memory.
<!-- @endparam -->
<!-- @param name="$y_gap" type="int" default="0" -->
Row offset of the visible area.
<!-- @endparam -->
<!-- @param name="$mirror_x" type="bool" default="false" -->
Mirror horizontally.
<!-- @endparam -->
<!-- @param name="$mirror_y" type="bool" default="false" -->
Mirror vertically.
<!-- @endparam -->
<!-- @param name="$swap_xy" type="bool" default="false" -->
Swap the axes; with a mirror this rotates the picture by 90 degrees. The driver does not swap `$width` and
`$height` for you: `fill()` and `rect()` clip to them as given, which matters only on a panel that is not square.
<!-- @endparam -->
<!-- @param name="$invert" type="bool" default="false" -->
Invert colours, for panels wired with inverted polarity.
<!-- @endparam -->
<!-- @endparams -->

On the first construction for a bus and CS, the driver:

1. allocates its band buffer (`width` x 40 pixels, DMA-capable internal RAM);
2. pulses reset (20 ms low, then 120 ms wait) when `$rst` is not `-1`;
3. reads the panel's ID register at 5 MHz to pick one of two init sequences (two silicon variants);
4. opens the `esp_lcd` panel IO on the bus's host at 40 MHz in quad mode, sends a software reset and the init
   sequence;
5. applies `swap_xy`, mirroring, gaps and inversion, turns the display on, then drives the backlight high.

The panel memory is not cleared: call `fill()` first. A later construction with the same bus and CS and the
same arguments returns the running panel; with any argument different it throws
`display on cs N already initialised with different parameters`.

Throws `\ValueError` when `$width` or `$height` is outside 1 to 4096, and `\Exception` with
`cannot reserve st77916 (cs=N): ...` (CS taken by another device, or no free device slot) or
`st77916 init failed: ...` (out of memory, or an `esp_lcd` error). After a failed init the next construction
tries again.

## fill()

<!-- @method name="St77916::fill(int $color): void" returns="void" visibility="public" -->

Paints the whole `width` x `height` area with `$color` (the low 16 bits are used). The driver fills its band
buffer once and sends it in bands of 40 rows, then waits until every band has gone out.

## rect()

<!-- @method name="St77916::rect(int $x, int $y, int $w, int $h, int $color): void" returns="void" visibility="public" -->

Paints the rectangle from (`$x`, `$y`), `$w` pixels wide and `$h` high, clipped to the panel. Origin is the
top-left corner. A rectangle entirely off the panel, or with a negative size, paints nothing and raises
nothing. Narrow rectangles go out in taller bands, so their cost is close to proportional to their area.

## rgb()

<!-- @method name="St77916::rgb(int $r, int $g, int $b): int" returns="int" visibility="public" -->

Packs 8-bit components (the low 8 bits of each) into RGB565: `(($r & 0xF8) << 8) | (($g & 0xFC) << 3) | (($b &
0xFF) >> 3)`. An instance method; it touches no hardware.

## wake()

<!-- @method name="St77916::wake(): void" returns="void" visibility="public" -->

Re-initialises a panel whose QSPI lines were disturbed by a light sleep: pulses reset (when `$rst` is not
`-1`), re-sends the init sequence and re-applies orientation, gaps and inversion, turns the display on and drives the
backlight high again.
The picture is not restored; repaint after it. See
[Errors, limits and light sleep](../reference/errors-and-limits.md).

## Inherited methods

`transfer()`, `write()` and `read()` from `Device` throw `SPI ... failed: ESP_ERR_INVALID_STATE` on this class:
the driver owns the wire through `esp_lcd`.

<!-- @code-block language="php" label="a framed panel" -->
```php
<?php
use Baremetal\Spi\Bus;
use Baremetal\Spi\Driver\St77916;

$lcd = new St77916(new Bus(sclk: 40, mosi: 46, miso: 45, data2: 42, data3: 41), cs: 21, rst: 3, bl: 5);

$black = $lcd->rgb(0, 0, 0);
$teal  = $lcd->rgb(0, 128, 128);

$lcd->fill($teal);
$lcd->rect(40, 40, 280, 280, $black);    // leaves a 40 px teal frame
```
